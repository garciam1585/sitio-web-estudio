Social Security Law

Services

Specialized legal advice on the Argentine pension system. I review your circumstances, explain the available options, and handle procedures and case follow-up.

Pension Planning

Planning ahead starts with reviewing your records. I analyze your work history, identify missing contributions or discrepancies, and advise on steps to address them. I assess retirement options according to your circumstances.

  • Work history analysis
  • Detection of missing contributions
  • Advice on correcting records
  • Retirement strategy
  • Assessment of retirement options

Retirement Benefits Management

Are you considering retirement but have gaps in your contribution record? I review your work history and assess the options that may apply to your circumstances. I advise on documentation, handle filing before ANSES, and follow up on the application.

  • Assessment of contribution gaps
  • Case filing
  • Complete follow-up
  • Procedures before ANSES
  • Application status monitoring

Survivor Pensions

After the loss of a family member, I advise on survivor pension applications, review the circumstances and supporting documents, and handle filing and follow-up before ANSES. I explain the steps and requirements relevant to each case.

  • Direct pensions
  • Derived pensions
  • For spouses and domestic partners
  • For minor children
  • Application follow-up

Benefit Readjustment

Do you have questions about how your retirement benefit was calculated or adjusted? I review the calculation, your records, and the legal grounds that may support a claim. I explain the available administrative or judicial options and assess whether a readjustment claim is appropriate.

  • Initial calculation analysis
  • Benefit mobility claims
  • Benefit updates
  • Difference claims
  • Assessment of potential arrears

Disability Retirement

If a health condition affects your ability to work, I review your contribution record and medical documentation to assess a possible disability retirement application. I advise on procedures before the Medical Commissions and on reviews or appeals where appropriate.

  • Contribution regularity calculation
  • Temporary retirement
  • Permanent retirement
  • Procedures before Medical Commissions
  • Appeals and reviews

Healthcare Injunctions

Do you have concerns about healthcare coverage when retiring? I review your coverage, documentation, and the response from your health insurer or private healthcare provider. I assess the available legal options, including whether an injunction action is appropriate for your circumstances.

  • Injunction actions
  • Health insurance coverage review
  • Private healthcare plan review
  • Assessment of coverage costs
  • Advice on coverage disputes

Other Services

I provide specialized advice on individual matters within the Argentine pension system, including service recognition, teacher retirement, special regimes, and international agreements.

  • Service Recognition
  • Teacher Retirement
  • Differential Regimes
  • Hazardous Work
  • International Agreement Retirement

About Me:
